# Receive a weekly report

A weekly report gives you the previous complete week's performance without opening the dashboard. Subscriptions are off by default and belong to each user.

## Enable a report

1. Open **Settings → Email reports** for the product.
2. Preview the report and check the product, recipient and timezone.
3. Enable your own report subscription and save.

Reports run on Monday at 09:00 in the product timezone and summarize the previous Monday–Sunday, with comparisons and source/country highlights. This differs from a rolling seven-day report that includes today.

## Stop or troubleshoot delivery

Disable the subscription in settings or use the signed unsubscribe link in a received email. If email is unavailable in the dashboard, contact the Jelto team; users do not configure Jelto's email delivery service.

If an expected email is missing, check the subscription, account email, spam folder and whether the scheduled time has passed in the product timezone. Use the preview to confirm the underlying report is available.
