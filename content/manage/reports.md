# Receive a weekly report

A weekly report gives you the previous complete week's performance without opening the dashboard. Subscriptions belong to each user. When you create a product, your own report for it starts on; people you invite to a product turn theirs on themselves.

## Enable a report

1. Open **Settings → Email reports** for the product.
2. Preview the report and check the product, recipient and timezone.
3. Enable your own report subscription and save.

Reports run on Monday at 09:00 in the product timezone and summarize the previous Monday–Sunday. Each report has:

- the week's visitors and your key metrics, with comparisons to the week before;
- top sources and top countries;
- for a website, a day-by-hour grid of pageviews with the busiest hour, and the most common browser, operating system and device;
- AI crawler requests, when server tracking is set up for the product: how often AI assistants requested your pages, which assistants, and which pages. These are requests, not visitors. Without server tracking, the report links its setup instead.

This differs from a rolling seven-day report that includes today. Reports pause while the account is not collecting data, for example after a trial ends, so a stopped week never reads as a drop in traffic.

## Stop or troubleshoot delivery

Disable the subscription in settings or use the signed unsubscribe link in a received email. A disabled report stays off until you enable it again. If email is unavailable in the dashboard, contact the Jelto team; users do not configure Jelto's email delivery service.

If an expected email is missing, check the subscription, account email, spam folder and whether the scheduled time has passed in the product timezone. Use the preview to confirm the underlying report is available.
