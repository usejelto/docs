# Explore and share product analytics

Use the dashboard to answer a specific question, then share only the result your audience needs.

## Explore your reports

- [Read your dashboard](../guides/read-your-dashboard.md): metrics, dates and comparisons.
- [Filter your data](../guides/filter-your-data.md) and [save a segment](../analytics/segments.md).
- [Understand traffic](../guides/understand-your-traffic.md) and [campaign links](../analytics/campaigns.md).
- [Understand app usage](../guides/understand-app-usage.md), [revenue](../guides/understand-revenue.md) and [bot activity](../guides/understand-bot-activity.md).

Above the overview chart, choose **Activity**, then Calendar, Day and hour or Live map. These views show aggregate activity. Choose **Trend** to return to the time series. Chart tooltips can stay open when clicked; Escape dismisses them.

## Choose presentation and interpretation

[Primary metric](../analytics/kpi.md) changes the overview's emphasis. **Settings → Analytics preferences → Chart palette** changes the chart and map colors. It does not change collected data.

Analytics preferences also choose the reporting currency and whether known renewals are included. Original payment amounts remain preserved; missing exchange rates can make converted totals incomplete. Named entry groups map registered hosts and path patterns to aggregate payment-entry labels; their order matters because the first matching group wins. A page-path report and an attributed payment-entry group have different coverage.

[Cookies and domains](../analytics/cookies-and-domains.md) explains why new/returning visitors or visit-count filters may be unavailable.
